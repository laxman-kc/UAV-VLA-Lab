The links above target only reusable source/docs and assets already verified as published in [Simulation MVP evidence v1](https://github.com/laxman-kc/UAV-VLA-Lab/releases/tag/simulation-mvp-evidence-v1). P01/P02/P05 and full phase bundles remain retained locally. The seven newer P08–P14 MP4 candidates require a separate exact-file publication decision; no future-release URL is invented here. The reviewed P10 candidate includes exact GPU-memory diagnostics, distinct from the already-published historical source-image video.

The source labels project body-x/body-z displacement and relative ZYX yaw over five recorded index increments. Physical seconds remain unknown; omitted lateral/tilt motion and controller quantization/execution differences remain explicit. All 25 stop flags are false. The separate privileged heading-restoration prototype was never executed or approved because its privileged objective is absent from the retained navigation prompt.

The final P12 candidate remained fixed through P13 and P14. Development and holdout results are small same-map descriptive comparisons: holdout SR rose by one mission while OSR fell by one. They do not establish broad or causal improvement, unseen-map generalization, learned recovery or physical touchdown. There was no DPO, PX4 integration or physical-UAV experiment.
