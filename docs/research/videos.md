# About the comparison videos

The [README contains two playable, 20-second summaries](../../README.md#watch-what-changed). Each shows a mission with a better outcome after training, a mission with a worse outcome, and the overall result.

**Before training** is the released, already pretrained model. **After training** is the same model after 25 additional updates. The labels describe each mission's recorded successful-stop result; a camera image alone does not establish success.

The summaries use selected recorded images, with simplified captions. They are not continuous or time-synchronized flight recordings. Display time is presentation time, not simulator time. The [source record](../assets/videos/sources.json) identifies the original files, selected frames, and short video files.
