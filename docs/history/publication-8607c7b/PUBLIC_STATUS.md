## What the source alignment establishes

For the selected examples, the target forward/down components match source body-x/body-z displacement from record `i` to `i+5`; yaw matches the first ZYX Euler component of the relative quaternion. All six raw record identities and current/future image hashes are retained in the private evidence, and the fixed numerical audit tolerance is `1e-9`. The additional 20 examples test this relationship under a separate predeclared selection.

The horizon is **five recorded index increments**, with unknown physical elapsed seconds and no established image/state acquisition simultaneity. Body-y translation and relative pitch/roll are omitted. Rotation-first execution, the large-yaw branch and quantization introduce differences from the source trajectory. Numerical alignment is not an exact controller replay or the released generator's complete specification. All 25 rows have both stop flags false, so the corpus provides no positive LAND examples.

The named reviewers are Codex agents. Approval is qualified ordinary-demonstration review, not human certification, newly collected expert corrections, guaranteed collision-free execution or learned recovery. The separate privileged heading-restoration prototype remains unapproved and unexecuted because its privileged goal is absent from the retained navigation prompt. See the [dated scope decision](docs/REFERENCE_ALIGNMENT_DECISION.md).

## Fixed candidate and completed comparison rule

P12 continued the released adapter and saved projector with a frozen BF16 base, FP32 trainable parameters/state, BF16 autocast and gradient checkpointing. AdamW used learning rate `2e-5`, seed `100`, batch/accumulation `1/1`, weight decay `0.03`, gradient clipping `1.0`, and no scheduler. The final configured step is the only candidate; there is no best-of search or continuation from the P10-updated checkpoint. No optimizer/RNG resume state is saved.

P13 reported the fixed candidate without a gain requirement. P14 retained **the same fixed final P12 candidate regardless of P13 outcome**. The holdout plan froze after the P13 result was reported; both actual arms executed fresh after that freeze. The later candidate receipt-path amendment did not change the protocol, cohort, candidate or one-attempt limit. No model switch or holdout tuning occurred in this cycle. [Comparison preparation](docs/runbooks/development_comparison_preparation.md), [comparison contract](docs/runbooks/navigation_comparison.md).

The benchmark task uses target information and a navigation stopping criterion; success does not demonstrate autonomous physical landing. Project train/development/holdout separation is enforced, but the released weights' complete original training history is not proven. Same-map small groups, unmeasured repeat-run variance and historical baseline reuse in P13 limit interpretation. Both comparisons reported zero invalid or missing pairs. Actual runtime warnings remain in retained private evidence.

## Completed cycle and public evidence

Development SR changed from 6/20 to 7/20; holdout SR changed from 3/10 to 4/10 while holdout OSR changed from 6/10 to 5/10. The combined evidence supports a completed, reproducible engineering pilot with mixed navigation results. Training loss reduction does not establish navigation improvement, and these observations do not isolate a causal training effect.
